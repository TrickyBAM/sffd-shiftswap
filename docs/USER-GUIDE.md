# ShiftSwap User Guide

ShiftSwap helps SFFD members trade shifts with each other: post a shift you need
covered, pick up someone else's, and keep track of who owes whom.

> **TeleStaff is still the official schedule.** ShiftSwap only helps you find a
> trade and keep track of it. Every trade still has to go through TeleStaff and
> be approved the normal SFFD way. You'll be asked to confirm you understand
> this the first time you sign in.

App address: **<https://sffd-shiftswap.vercel.app>**

---

## 1. Getting started

1. Open the app address on your phone and tap **Sign up**. Use your email and
   pick a password.
2. Fill in your profile: full name, phone, rank, station and tour (choose
   **No tour** if you're relief, detail or 40-hour). **Enter your employee ID**
   too. It's the surest way to be approved instantly.
3. If your details match the department roster, you're in right away.
   Otherwise an admin will check and may call or text you. You'll get in as soon
   as they approve you.
4. Read the TeleStaff notice and tap to accept it. You only do this once.

**Forgot your password?** There is no reset email. Ask a ShiftSwap admin. They
give you a temporary password, and you choose a new one when you sign in.

---

## 2. Install it on your phone

Installing puts ShiftSwap on your home screen like a normal app. **On iPhone,
alerts only work once it's installed.**

**iPhone (iOS 16.4 or later):**

1. Open the app address in **Safari** (not Chrome or another browser).
2. Tap the **Share** button (the square with an arrow pointing up).
3. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.
4. From now on, open ShiftSwap from the new home-screen icon.

**Android:**

1. Open the app address in **Chrome**.
2. Tap **Install** if a banner offers it. Otherwise tap the **⋮** menu, then
   **Install app** or **Add to Home screen**.
3. Open ShiftSwap from the new icon.

---

## 3. Turn on alerts

1. Open ShiftSwap from the home-screen icon.
2. Go to **Profile** and turn **Alerts on this device** on. When your phone
   asks, tap **Allow**.
3. Choose which **new shifts** you want to hear about: **My station**, **My
   battalion** (the default), **My division**, **Everyone**, or **Off**. You only
   hear about shifts you could actually take (same rank, and you're off that day).

You always get alerts about your own posts and trades: requests, confirmations,
cancel requests and messages. They also appear under the **bell** at the top of
every screen, even when phone alerts are off. Turn alerts on for each phone or
computer you use.

---

## 4. Your calendar

The **Calendar** tab shows your month. The legend is always on screen:

| Color | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Red** | You're working (one of your tour days) |
| **Red outline**, "Covered by …" | Your tour day that someone else is covering. You're off. |
| **Orange** | Your shift that's posted and waiting for someone |
| **Blue** (with a number) | Open shifts you could pick up that day |
| **Gray** | You're covering someone's shift |
| **Purple** | A SwapMatch day (either direction) |

Tap any day to see what you can do: **Post this shift**, **See available
shifts**, or **View trade**.

---

## 5. Post a shift you need covered

1. Tap **Post** (or tap one of your red days on the Calendar).
2. Pick the day. You can only post your own tour days (members with no tour can
   pick any future date), up to 180 days ahead.
3. Choose the shift type:
   - **24-Hour**: 08:00 to 08:00 the next day
   - **PM**: 16:00 to 08:00 the next day
4. The location defaults to your station. Change it if needed.
5. Choose **who can take it**: Anyone, My Division, My Battalion or My Station.
6. Optional: add **SwapMatch** return dates (see below) and a short note.
7. Tap **Post**. Members who can take it are alerted.

You can cancel your post any time until someone is confirmed.

### SwapMatch: a shift for a shift

Want a day back instead of owing one? Add up to **10 return dates**: days
you're off and could work for the other person. Whoever takes your shift picks
one of those dates. When you confirm them, ShiftSwap records **both** trades
together: they work your day, and you work theirs.

---

## 6. Ask for someone else's shift

1. Open the **Board**. It starts with your battalion and your rank. Use the
   filters (division, battalion, station) to see more. Keep **Only shifts I can
   take** on to hide ones you can't.
2. Tap a shift, then **Request**.
3. For a SwapMatch, **pick the return date** you'll give back. It must be one of
   your own working days.
4. Add a short message if you like, and send.

If you can't take a shift, the app tells you why, for example "You're working
that day" or "Trades are same rank only".

The poster gets your request and picks one person. You'll get an alert either
way. You can withdraw your request while it's still waiting.

---

## 7. Confirm who covers your shift

1. When someone asks for your shift, you get an alert. Open **Trades ▸ Pending**
   (or the shift itself).
2. Look at each request. Tap a name to see their rank, station and trust
   score.
3. Tap **Confirm** on the one you want. Everyone else is told, politely, that
   the shift was filled. Or tap **Decline** on a request you don't want.

Once confirmed:

- Open the trade to **call or text** your partner and to **chat**. Phone numbers
  and emails are only shown to trade partners.
- Tap **Copy trade summary** to paste the details (names, ranks, stations, date,
  hours, return date) into your paperwork.
- **Enter the trade in TeleStaff** as SFFD policy requires.

---

## 8. Backing out of a trade (both people must agree)

Plans change. A confirmed trade can only be undone if **both of you agree**,
before the shift starts:

1. Open the trade and tap **Ask to cancel**, with a short reason.
2. Your partner gets an alert and chooses **Agree** or **Decline**.
   - **Agree**: the trade is undone. If the shift hasn't started, it goes back on
     the Board as an open post. A SwapMatch is undone on both days.
   - **Decline**: the trade stays as it is.
3. Changed your mind? You can withdraw your ask while it's waiting.

Once a shift has started, only an admin can void the trade. If you undo a trade
that was already in TeleStaff, fix it in TeleStaff too.

---

## 9. Balances and your profile

- **Trades ▸ Balances** shows, for each person you've traded with, who owes
  whom, split by 24-Hour and PM (for example "Mike owes you 1 × 24-Hour").
  SwapMatch trades cancel out automatically.
- **Profile** shows your totals: **Posted**, **Covered** (shifts you worked for
  others), **Given** (shifts others worked for you), **Outstanding** (your open
  posts) and **Balance** (Covered − Given).
- Your **trust score** goes up when you cover shifts, and down a little if you
  leave old posts open for more than a week. Posters can see it when they choose
  between requests.
- On Profile you can also change your phone, station and tour, change your
  password and sign out. Ask an admin to fix your name, rank or employee ID.

---

## 10. Add ShiftSwap to your phone's calendar

Your work days and trades can show up in Apple Calendar, Google Calendar or
Outlook, and they update on their own.

1. Go to **Profile** and find the calendar section.
2. **iPhone:** tap **Add to my calendar**. The Calendar app opens. Tap
   **Subscribe**, then **Add**.
   **Google Calendar:** tap **Copy** next to the calendar link. On a computer,
   open Google Calendar, choose **Other calendars ▸ + ▸ From URL**, and paste it.
   **Outlook:** copy the link the same way, then choose Add calendar ▸ Subscribe
   from web, and paste it.

Calendar apps refresh subscriptions on their own schedule: iPhone within hours,
Google sometimes only once a day. ShiftSwap itself is always up to date.

**Keep the link private.** Anyone who has it can see your work days. If you
shared it by mistake, tap **Reset link** on your Profile. The old link stops
working, and you add the new one to your calendar again.

---

## 11. Good to know

- **No signal?** ShiftSwap shows the last calendar, board and trades it loaded,
  with a yellow **Offline snapshot** ribbon and the time it was saved. You can't
  post or request until you're back online.
- **Same rank only.** You can only trade with members of your own rank.
- **Nothing changes once a shift starts.** Posts, requests, confirmations and
  cancellations all close when the shift begins.
- **Privacy:** other members never see your phone or email unless you're trading
  with them. Admins can see member details to approve and help people.
- **Problems or questions?** Ask a ShiftSwap admin.
