# ShiftSwap User Guide

ShiftSwap helps SFFD members trade shifts with each other: post a shift you need
covered, pick up someone else's, and keep track of who owes whom.

> **TeleStaff is still the official schedule.** ShiftSwap only helps you find a
> trade and keep track of it. Every trade still has to go through TeleStaff and
> be approved the normal SFFD way. You'll be asked to confirm you understand
> this the first time you sign in.

App address: **<https://sffd-shiftswap.vercel.app>**

Button and screen names in **bold** are exactly what you see in the app.

---

## 1. Getting started

1. Open the app address on your phone and tap **Create an account**. Enter your
   full name (as it appears on the department roster), email, mobile phone and a
   password of at least 8 characters, then tap **Create account**.
2. Fill in your profile: rank, station and tour (choose **No tour (relief,
   detail, 40-hour)** if that's you). **Enter your employee ID** too. It's the
   surest way to be approved instantly.
3. If your details match the department roster, you're in right away.
   Otherwise an admin will check and may call or text you. You'll get in as soon
   as they approve you.
4. Read the TeleStaff notice, tick **I understand** and tap **Continue**. You only
   do this once.

**Forgot your password?** There is no reset email. Ask a ShiftSwap admin. They
give you a temporary password, and you choose a new one when you log in.

---

## 2. Install it on your phone

Installing puts ShiftSwap on your home screen like a normal app. **On iPhone,
alerts only work once it's installed.**

**iPhone (iOS 16.4 or later):**

1. Open the app address in **Safari** (not Chrome or another browser).
2. Tap the **Share** button (the square with an arrow pointing up).
3. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.
4. From now on, open ShiftSwap from the new home-screen icon.

**Android:**

1. Open the app address in **Chrome**.
2. Tap **Install** if a banner offers it. Otherwise tap the **⋮** menu, then
   **Install app** or **Add to Home screen**.
3. Open ShiftSwap from the new icon.

---

## 3. Turn on alerts

1. Open ShiftSwap from the home-screen icon.
2. Go to **Profile** and, under **Alerts**, turn **Alerts on this device** on.
   When your phone asks, tap **Allow**.
3. Next to **New-shift alerts**, tap **Change** and choose which new shifts you
   want to hear about: **Off**, **My Station**, **My Battalion** (the default),
   **My Division** or **Everywhere**. You only hear about shifts you could
   actually take (same rank, on a day you're off).

You always get alerts about your own posts and trades: requests, confirmations,
cancel requests and messages. They also appear under the **bell** at the top of
every screen, even when phone alerts are off. Turn alerts on for each phone or
computer you use.

---

## 4. Your calendar

The **Calendar** tab shows your month. The key is always on screen:

| Key | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **You're on duty** (red) | One of your tour days: you're working |
| **Your open post** (orange) | Your shift that's posted and waiting for someone |
| **Open shifts you could take** (blue, with a number) | How many open shifts you could pick up that day |
| **You're covering someone** (gray) | You're working someone else's shift |
| **SwapMatch day** (purple) | A day in a SwapMatch trade (either direction) |
| **Someone covers your shift** (red outline) | Your tour day that someone else is covering. You're off. |
| **You work 0800–1600, PM covered** (red outline with a red bar) | You gave away only the PM part (16:00 to 08:00). You still work 0800–1600. |
| **Today** (white dot) | Today's date |

Tap any day to see what's happening and what you can do: **Post this shift**
(or **Post a shift on this day** if you have no tour), **See N available
shifts**, **View your post** or **View trade**.

The blue number counts shifts you could take anywhere in the department, not
just your battalion. **See N available shifts** opens the Board on that day
with all locations, so you see the same shifts that were counted.

## 5. Post a shift you need covered

1. Tap **Post** (or tap one of your red days on the Calendar, then **Post this
   shift**).
2. Under **Your shift**, pick the day. You can only post your own tour days
   (members with no tour can pick any future date), up to 180 days ahead.
3. Choose the **Shift type**:
   - **24-Hour**: 08:00 to 08:00 the next day
   - **PM**: 16:00 to 08:00 the next day. You still work 0800–1600 that day.
4. **Location** defaults to your station. Change it if needed.
5. Optional: under **Shift back**, turn on **Want a shift back? (SwapMatch)**
   and pick days from **Your days off** (see below).
6. Choose **Who can take it**: **Anyone**, **My Division**, **My Battalion** or
   **My Station**.
7. Optional: add **Notes** for the person taking it.
8. Tap **Post shift**. Members who can take it get an alert right away.

You can cancel your post any time until someone is confirmed: open it and tap
**Cancel my post**. Anyone who asked for it is told.

### SwapMatch: a shift for a shift

Want a day back instead of owing one? Add up to **10 return dates**: days
you're off and could work for the other person. Whoever takes your shift picks
one of those dates. When you confirm them, ShiftSwap records **both** trades
together: they work your day, and you work theirs.

## 6. Ask for someone else's shift

1. Open the **Board**. It starts with your battalion and your rank. Tap the
   filter button next to **Only shifts I can take** to pick a different
   division, battalion, station or rank. Keep **Only shifts I can take** on to
   hide shifts you can't take.
2. Tap a shift to open it. ShiftSwap checks whether you can take it.
3. For a SwapMatch, **pick the return date** you'll give back. It must be one of
   your own working days.
4. Add a message to the poster if you like, then tap **Send request**.

If you can't take a shift, the app tells you why, for example "You're working
that day" or "Trades are same rank only".

The poster gets your request and picks one person. You'll get an alert either
way. While it's still waiting, you can tap **Withdraw my request**.

---

## 7. Confirm who covers your shift

1. When someone asks for your shift, you get an alert. Open **Trades ▸ Pending**
   (or the shift itself).
2. Look at each request. It shows the person's rank, station, when they asked,
   their trust score and how many shifts they've covered and given, plus their
   message and, for a SwapMatch, the day you'd work for them. Tap **Message**
   (with their name) to chat with them before you decide.
3. Tap **Confirm** on the one you want, then **Confirm trade**. Everyone else is
   told, politely, that the shift was filled. Or tap **Decline**, then **Decline
   request**, on a request you don't want.

Once confirmed:

- Open the trade to **Call**, **Text** or **Email** your partner and to chat.
  Phone numbers and emails are only shown to trade partners.
- Under **Paperwork**, tap **Copy trade summary** to paste the details (names,
  ranks, stations, date, hours, return date) into your paperwork.
- **Enter the trade in TeleStaff** as SFFD policy requires.

---

## 8. Backing out of a trade (both people must agree)

Plans change. A confirmed trade can only be undone if **both of you agree**,
before the shift starts:

1. Open the trade and tap **Ask to cancel this trade**. Add a reason if you
   like (your partner sees it), then tap **Send cancel request**.
2. Your partner gets an alert and taps **Agree to cancel** (then **Yes, cancel
   it**) or **Decline**.
   - **Agree**: the trade is undone. The shift goes back to the person who
     posted it and back on the Board as an open post. A SwapMatch is undone on
     both days.
   - **Decline**: the trade stays as it is.
3. Changed your mind? Tap **Withdraw my cancel request** while it's waiting.

Undone trades stay listed under **Trades ▸ History**. Once a shift has started,
it can't be cancelled in ShiftSwap anymore; contact an admin, who can void it.
If you undo a trade that was already in TeleStaff, fix it in TeleStaff too.

## 9. Balances and your profile

- **Trades ▸ Balances** shows your totals (**Covered**, **Given**, **Balance**)
  and, for each person you've traded with, who owes whom, split by 24-Hour and
  PM (for example "Mike Lee owes you 1 × 24-Hour" or "You owe Ana Cruz 1 × PM").
  SwapMatch trades cancel out automatically.
- **Profile ▸ Your stats** shows **Posted**, **Covered** (shifts you worked for
  others), **Given** (shifts others worked for you), **Outstanding** (your open
  posts) and **Balance** (Covered − Given).
- Your **trust score** starts at 100. It drops 5 points for each of your open
  posts that has waited more than a week, and each shift you covered in the last
  30 days adds 3 back (up to 100). Posters see it when they choose between
  requests.
- On **Profile**, tap **Edit my details** to change your phone, station and
  tour. Under **Account** you can **Change password** and **Sign out** (signing
  out turns off alerts on that device and clears the data saved on it). Ask an
  admin to fix your name, rank or employee ID.

## 10. Add ShiftSwap to your phone's calendar

Your work days and trades can show up in Apple Calendar, Google Calendar or
Outlook, and they update on their own.

1. Go to **Profile ▸ Calendar**.
2. **iPhone:** tap **Add to my calendar**. The Calendar app opens. Tap
   **Subscribe**, then **Add**.
3. **Google Calendar:** tap **Copy calendar link**. On a computer (or in your
   phone's browser with Desktop site turned on), open Google Calendar, choose
   **+** next to **Other calendars**, then **From URL**, paste the link and
   choose **Add calendar**. It then shows in the Google Calendar app too.
4. **Outlook or another app:** tap **Subscribe with this link**, or paste the
   copied link where the app asks for a calendar URL (in Outlook: Add calendar ▸
   Subscribe from web).

Calendar apps refresh subscriptions on their own schedule: iPhone within hours,
Google sometimes only once a day. ShiftSwap itself is always up to date.

**Keep the link private.** Anyone who has it can see your work days. If you
shared it by mistake, tap **Reset link** in the Calendar section. The old link
stops working, and you add the new one to your calendar again.

---

## 11. Good to know

- **No signal?** ShiftSwap shows the last calendar, board and trades it loaded,
  with a yellow **Offline snapshot** ribbon and the time it was saved. You can't
  post or request until you're back online.
- **Same rank only.** You can only trade with members of your own rank.
- **Nothing changes once a shift starts.** Posts, requests, confirmations and
  cancellations all close when the shift begins.
- **Privacy:** other members never see your phone or email unless you're trading
  with them. Admins can see member details to approve and help people. The full
  notice is on **Profile ▸ Privacy & disclaimer**.
- **Leaving ShiftSwap?** Ask an admin to remove your account. Your phone, email
  and employee ID are erased and you can't sign in anymore; your name stays on
  past trades so other members' history still adds up. Details are on the
  privacy page.
- **Problems or questions?** Ask a ShiftSwap admin.
